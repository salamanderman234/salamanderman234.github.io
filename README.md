# Personal Portfolio — Minimalist & Swiss Design

A personal developer & designer portfolio built with semantic HTML, CSS Grid, and vanilla JavaScript, designed for zero-build hosting on **GitHub Pages**.

## Features
- **Personal Identity Hero**: Includes a Swiss portrait/monogram frame (supports your own photo via `avatarUrl` or auto-generates a geometric monogram), availability status badge, quick facts, and Resume/CV button.
- **01 / About Me & Toolkit**: Personal biography, key stats (experience, shipped projects, commits), and a categorized technical skills matrix.
- **02 / Featured Projects**: Filterable personal & open-source projects with direct **Live Demo**, **Code (GitHub)**, and **Details Modal** buttons.
- **03 / Experience & Education**: Expandable timeline of your work history and academic background.
- **04 / Contact & Quick Message Composer**: Direct copy-email button, social links, and a static-friendly message form that dispatches pre-formatted emails without needing a backend server.
- **Swiss Grid & Dark Mode**: Press `G` to toggle the 12-column Swiss grid overlay or `T` to switch between Paper (Light) and Carbon (Dark) themes.

## How to Make It Yours
Open `script.js` and edit `PORTFOLIO_DATA` at the top of the file:
1. **`profile`**: Set your `name`, `initials`, `role`, `location`, `email`, `resumeUrl` (e.g. `"./resume.pdf"`), and `avatarUrl` (e.g. `"./photo.jpg"`).
2. **`about` & `skills`**: Update your personal bio paragraphs, stats, and tech stack tags.
3. **`projects` & `experience`**: Add your own projects, GitHub links, work experience, and university degree.
4. **`socials`**: Update your GitHub, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and Instagram URLs.
